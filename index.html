CHAPTER 01 — THE BEGINNING

It started with you.
And slowly, it became us.

I don't know when you became such an important part of my life. When your words became a part of my days, or when your happiness started feeling like my own.

I kept choosing you, even in moments when choosing you meant ignoring the questions I was too afraid to ask.

Maybe, to me, it was never just love. ♡

CHAPTER 03 — THE TRUTH

I was just a girl...
trying to believe.

I wasn't blind to every possibility. Maybe the doubts were there all along. But attachment has a strange way of making you silence the very thoughts that are trying to protect you.

People had their opinions. I heard them, but I kept hoping that what I felt was real, that what we had would turn out differently.

Perhaps I wasn't protecting our love anymore. Perhaps I was protecting the future I had imagined.

I believed. Even when I wasn't sure. ♡

Two hearts.
One uncertain future.

CHAPTER 04 — THE QUESTION THAT HURTS

Who am I standing beside...
if courage is what love needs?

Sometimes, I wonder who I was trusting so blindly. The thought of building a future with someone who might not have the courage to stand beside me leaves me questioning everything I once believed in.

Loving someone is one thing. Standing beside that love when life becomes complicated is another. I don't want to discover, at the hardest moment, that I was the only one ready to fight for us.

What hurts most is how long I kept pushing those questions aside. I was so attached to the person I loved that I forgot to ask whether I felt secure in the future I was imagining.

Sometimes, I wonder whether I loved with my eyes open... or hoped so hard that I stopped seeing clearly.

CHAPTER 06 — BUT NOT ANYMORE

I am not the same girl...
at least, I am learning.

Sometimes, I am angry with myself. Why did I keep believing when people around me had questions? Why did I silence my own doubts just because I was afraid of losing someone?

I called myself foolish for not listening. But loving someone deeply doesn't make a person foolish. Forgetting your own worth while trying to hold on to someone is what I am learning to change.

I want to build my career, grow into the person I dream of being, and stop measuring my worth by someone else's choices. I can care about someone without abandoning myself.

I can love you... and still choose myself.

Becoming her...

ONE LAST THING I WANT YOU TO KNOW

This was never
just a website.

Some things are difficult to say out loud. So I left them here, between the words.

You may never fully know how attached I became, how many times I tried to quiet my doubts, or how often I held on because I believed there was something worth protecting between us.

I never expected you to be perfect. I wanted honesty, clarity, and the feeling that I wasn't the only one thinking about our future. I wanted love that felt safe, not a future I had to guess at.

Sometimes, I feel angry at myself for ignoring my own questions. Sometimes, I wonder whether I was too hopeful, too attached, or simply afraid to lose what I had imagined we could become.

I am not writing this to prove you wrong. I am writing it because my feelings were real, even in the moments when I didn't know what to do with them.

I still have memories. I still have feelings. But I also have dreams of my own, a life to build, and a version of myself I don't want to lose again.

What I felt was real.
What comes next must be real, too. ♡

— The girl who felt everything.
